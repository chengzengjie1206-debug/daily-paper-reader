## 摘要
我们提出了FlareEUV，这是一种多模态深度学习框架，用于利用NASA太阳动力学观测站（SDO）的多仪器观测数据，预测显著太阳耀斑期间连续三天的每日极紫外（EUV）辐照度（6.5纳米）。我们考虑了2011年至2014年第24太阳活动周期间的33次显著耀斑。SDO观测包括13张共对准的全盘图像，涵盖8个AIA EUV/UV和5个HMI磁/连续谱产品。FlareEUV利用基于轻量级注意力机制的架构，从原始成像数据中学习磁结构和日冕发射之间的关系。我们的实验结果表明，FlareEUV在显著耀斑期间的短期EUV辐照度预测中表现良好，并优于基线方法。

## Abstract
We present FlareEUV, a multimodal deep learning framework for predicting daily extreme ultraviolet (EUV) irradiance at 6.5 nm over three consecutive days during significant solar flares, using multi-instrument observations from NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO). We consider 33 significant flares in the period between 2011 and 2014 in Solar Cycle 24. The SDO observations include 13 co-aligned full-disk images, comprising eight AIA EUV/UV and five HMI magnetic/continuum products. FlareEUV learns the relationship between magnetic structure and coronal emission from the raw imaging data using a lightweight attention-based architecture. Our experimental results demonstrate the good performance of FlareEUV in short-term EUV irradiance forecasting during the significant flares and its superiority over baseline methods.

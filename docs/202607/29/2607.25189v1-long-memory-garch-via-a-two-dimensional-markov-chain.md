## 摘要
本文提出一种GARCH类型的波动率模型，其中潜在幂律核的水平与斜率更新在二维马尔可夫状态下产生过去冲击的状态依赖衰减。我们推导了一个联合Foster-Lyapunov条件，并建立了正Harris遍历性和不变分布的唯一性。模拟显示，对数平方新息中存在显著的低频持续性，特别是在诊断稳定性边界附近。实证上，该模型捕捉了观察到的波动率持续性的很大一部分，并且仅使用二维马尔可夫状态就提供了具有竞争力的样本外预测精度。

## Abstract
This paper proposes a GARCH-type volatility model in which level-and-slope updates of a latent power-law kernel generate state-dependent decay of past shocks within a two-dimensional Markov state. We derive a joint Foster--Lyapunov condition and establish positive Harris recurrence and uniqueness of the invariant distribution. Simulations show substantial low-frequency persistence in log-squared innovations, especially near the diagnostic stability boundary. Empirically, the model captures a substantial portion of observed volatility persistence and delivers competitive out-of-sample forecast accuracy using only a two-dimensional Markov state.

## 摘要
概率预报评估本质上是多目标的，然而现有的适当评分规则将预测性能简化为单一标量值，可能掩盖预报集中度与预测准确性之间的权衡。我们引入了区间评分接收者操作特征（IS-ROC）曲线，这是一种图形化框架，表示通过改变预测紧密度所生成的完整区间预报族。我们证明了由数据生成过程诱导的IS-ROC曲线是帕累托最优且凸的，从而提供了最优预报前沿的几何刻画。基于这些性质，我们提出了一种基于切线与凸化的几何校准程序，以及一种通过凸包构造来组合竞争预报器的集成策略。最后，我们提供了一个实用的工作流程和数值示例，以说明在所提出框架内进行预报比较、校准和集成构建的方法。

## Abstract
Probabilistic forecast evaluation is inherently multi-objective, yet existing proper scoring rules reduce predictive performance to a single scalar value, potentially obscuring the trade-off between forecast concentration and predictive accuracy. We introduce the Interval-Score Receiver Operating Characteristic (IS-ROC) Curve, a graphical framework that represents the complete family of interval forecasts generated by varying prediction tightness. We show that the IS-ROC Curve induced by the data generating process is Pareto optimal and convex, providing a geometric characterization of the optimal forecasting frontier. Building on these properties, we propose a geometry-based calibration procedure based on tangent optimization and convexification, together with an ensemble strategy that combines competing forecasters through convex hull construction. Finally, we provide a practical workflow and numerical examples illustrating forecast comparison, calibration, and ensemble construction within the proposed framework.

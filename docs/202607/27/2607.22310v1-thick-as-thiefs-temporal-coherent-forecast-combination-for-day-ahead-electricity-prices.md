## 摘要
日前电价由多个竞争模型以及从每小时价格到区块和基荷产品的多个时间粒度进行预测。由此产生的预测存在两个不同的问题：不同粒度生成的预测是不一致的，因为加总结果与其组成部分的平均值不匹配，并且没有一个模型在所有市场、时期和水平上都是最准确的。我们开发了一种时序一致组合方法，一步解决这两个问题，将竞争专家在时间层次的所有层级上产生的预测汇集到一个单一的预测中，该预测满足加总约束，并且在线性无偏组合中具有最小误差方差。由于最优解依赖于高维误差协方差矩阵，我们比较了通过将相关结构与线性和非线性收缩方法交叉得到的不同估计量。利用四种模型类别对德国和西班牙日前市场的基础预测，组合预测在几乎所有时间层级上显著优于基础预测和最佳协调专家。

## Abstract
Day-ahead electricity prices are forecast by many competing models and at several temporal granularities, from hourly prices to block and baseload products. The resulting forecasts suffer from two distinct problems: forecasts produced at different granularities are incoherent, as the aggregates do not match the averages of their components, and no single model is the most accurate in every market, period and level. We develop a temporal coherent combination approach that addresses both problems in one step, pooling the forecasts that the competing experts produce at all the levels of a temporal hierarchy into a single forecast that satisfies the aggregation constraints and has minimum error variance among the linear unbiased combinations. Since the optimal solution depends on a high-dimensional error covariance matrix, we compare different estimators obtained by crossing correlation structures with linear and nonlinear shrinkage approaches. Using the base forecasts of four model classes for the German and Spanish day-ahead markets, the combined forecasts significantly outperform both the base forecasts and the best reconciled expert at nearly every temporal level.

## 摘要
理解大气污染物排放与其多重决定因素之间的复杂关系面临双重挑战：驱动因素在根本不同的时间分辨率上运行，从连续监测的气象变量到每年报告的社会经济指标，并且它们之间的相互联系随时间动态演变。为应对这些挑战，我们提出了一种部分函数动态结构方程模型（PFDSEM），该模型将函数型协变量（如高频气象数据）和标量预测变量（如经济和人口指标）连贯地整合到一个统一的动态结构框架中。该模型通过条件自回归（CAR）结构结合区域化线性模型（LMC）捕捉非平稳时间依赖性和变量间相关性，同时通过贝叶斯P样条平滑的基扩展引入函数型协变量。通过马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛进行贝叶斯推断提供了完整的量化不确定性。全面的模拟研究证实了在不同条件下参数恢复的准确性和对先验设定的稳健性。将PFDSEM应用于2015-2020年中国30个省份的污染物排放数据，我们识别出十大类社会-环境因素与十大主要空气污染物（包括CO₂）之间的时间动态和省份特异性关联。结果揭示了这些关联的强度和方向存在显著的跨省异质性，以及气象效应的明显季节性模式，为设计时间自适应和区域针对性的环境政策提供了定量证据基础。

## Abstract
Understanding the complex relationships between atmospheric pollutant emissions and their multifaceted determinants presents a dual challenge: driving factors operate at fundamentally different temporal resolutions, from continuously monitored meteorological variables to annually reported socio-economic indicators, and their interconnections evolve dynamically over time. To address these challenges, we propose a Partially Functional Dynamic Structural Equation Model (PFDSEM) that coherently integrates functional covariates (e.g., high-frequency meteorological data) and scalar predictors (e.g., economic and demographic indicators) within a unified dynamic structural framework. The model captures non-stationary temporal dependencies and inter-variable correlations via a Conditional Autoregressive (CAR) structure combined with a Linear Model of Coregionalization (LMC), while functional covariates are incorporated through basis expansion with Bayesian P-spline smoothing. Bayesian inference via Markov Chain Monte Carlo provides full uncertainty quantification. Comprehensive simulation studies confirm accurate parameter recovery and robustness to prior specifications under diverse conditions. Applying the PFDSEM to pollutant emissions data from 30 Chinese provinces (2015--2020), we identify temporally dynamic and province-specific associations between ten categories of socio-environmental factors and ten major air pollutants, including CO$_2$. The results reveal substantial cross-province heterogeneity in the strength and direction of these associations and pronounced seasonal patterns in meteorological effects, offering a quantitative evidence base for designing temporally adaptive and regionally tailored environmental policies.

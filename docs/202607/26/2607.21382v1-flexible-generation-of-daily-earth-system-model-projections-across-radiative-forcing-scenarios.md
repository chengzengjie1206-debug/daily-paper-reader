## 摘要
地球系统模型（ESM）对气候系统对人为强迫响应的预测，是评估气候变化影响并为适应和减缓政策提供信息的核心。然而，由于计算成本高昂，预测仅针对有限的一组标准化强迫情景（如共享社会经济路径，SSPs）且时间范围有限，时空分辨率对于直接影响评估仍然过低，且不确定性无法全面量化。近期数据驱动模型为天气预报提供了高效且准确的高分辨率模拟，但无法外推到未来温室气体浓度，因为它们无法捕捉对前所未有的强迫的响应，限制了其在气候变化预测中的价值。在这里，我们将响应理论与定制化的生成式机器学习框架相结合来应对这一挑战。我们的方法从月尺度的低分辨率ESM场中提取对辐射强迫的物理强迫响应，并利用该响应指导生成模型推断一致的每日全球高分辨率温度和降水预测。我们的概率方法可跨ESM泛化，并以高时空分辨率提供长期、偏差校正的辐射强迫响应。它高效生成了不确定性量化所需的大型集合，有效填补了现有SSPs之间的空白，并可将气候预测扩展到2300年及以后。因此，我们的框架通过提供跨排放情景的高效、稳定且高时空分辨率的长期气候预测集合，补充了ESM预测，从而实现了详细的冲击评估和对长期气候承诺的探索。

## Abstract
Earth system model (ESM) projections of the climate system's response to anthropogenic forcing are central to assess the impacts of climate change and inform adaptation and mitigation policies. However, given their high computational cost, projections are only made for a limited set of standardized forcing scenarios with limited temporal extent, such as the Shared Socioeconomic Pathways (SSPs), the spatiotemporal resolution remains too low for direct impact assessments, and uncertainties cannot be comprehensively quantified. Recent data-driven models offer efficient and accurate high-resolution simulations for weather prediction, but cannot extrapolate to future greenhouse gas concentrations because they cannot capture the responses to unprecedented forcing, limiting their value for climate change projections. Here, we combine response theory with a tailored generative machine learning framework to address this challenge. Our approach extracts the physical forced response to radiative forcing from monthly low-resolution ESM fields, and uses this response to guide a generative model to infer consistent daily global high-resolution temperature and precipitation projections. Our probabilistic approach generalizes across ESMs and provides long-term, bias-corrected responses to radiative forcing at high spatiotemporal resolution. It efficiently generates large ensembles needed for uncertainty quantification, effectively fills the gaps between existing SSPs, and readily extends climate projections to 2300 and beyond. Our framework hence complements ESM projections by providing efficient, stable, and high spatiotemporal resolution long-term climate projection ensembles across emission scenarios, enabling detailed impact assessment and exploration of long-term climate commitment.

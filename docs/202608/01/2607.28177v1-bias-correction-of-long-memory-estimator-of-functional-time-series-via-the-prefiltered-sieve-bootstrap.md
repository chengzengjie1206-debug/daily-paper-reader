## 摘要
我们研究了一种基于筛子自助法的偏差校正程序，用于估计平稳或非平稳分数积分过程中的长记忆参数d。该重采样方法对通过长记忆参数初步估计预滤波的数据实施筛子自助法。对于初始估计，我们推荐Frederiksen等人(2012)提出的带噪声的局部多项式Whittle (LPWN)估计量，以减少偏差，特别是在存在强短程自回归依赖时。通过一系列模拟研究，我们首先使用局部Whittle或去趋势波动分析估计量来突出偏差问题。然后，我们考虑LPWN估计量，并展示其筛子自助增强所实现的潜在偏差改进。作为副产品，筛子自助法还可以提供记忆参数的置信区间。

## Abstract
We investigate a bias correction procedure based on sieve bootstrapping to estimate the long-memory parameter d in stationary or nonstationary fractionally integrated processes. The resampling method implements a sieve bootstrap method on data prefiltered by a preliminary estimate of the long-memory parameter. For the initial estimate, we recommend the local polynomial Whittle with noise (LPWN) estimator in Frederiksen et al. (2012) to reduce bias, especially in the presence of a strong short-range autoregressive dependence. Through a series of simulation studies, we first highlight the issue of bias, using the local Whittle or detrended fluctuation analysis estimator. Then, we consider the LPWN estimator and show the potential improvement in bias achieved by its sieve bootstrap enhancement. As a byproduct, the sieve bootstrap can also provide confidence intervals of the memory parameter.
